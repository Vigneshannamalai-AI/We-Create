What it does

Enter it once. Everything else follows.

Every entry posts double-entry by itself to the ledgers, GST, TDS, TCS, bills outstanding and stock.

Scan a bill

Take a photo or upload a PDF. It reads the GSTINs, invoice number, date, HSN, taxable value, GST and total, decides from the GSTINs whether it's a purchase or a sale, finds or creates the party, and attaches the file. You review and save.

GST, done properly

  • CGST + SGST or IGST from place of supply
  • Exports and SEZ under LUT, reverse charge, blocked credit
  • GSTR-1 with portal JSON, GSTR-3B with Rule 88A set-off
  • GSTR-2B import and matching, IMS actions
  • E-invoice (INV-01) and e-way bill

Statements an auditor accepts

Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet in Schedule III with previous-year figures, Cash Flow (indirect, AS 3), Trial Balance, Day Book, ledgers, ageing, registers, HSN summary and stock.

TDS & TCS under the new Act

  • Income-tax Act 2025 payment codes, old sections shown alongside (1027 = old 194J(b))
  • Thresholds, and 20% where there's no PAN
  • TCS on sales
  • Challans tracked, with interest on late deposits
  • Quarterly returns prepared: Form 140 (TDS) and Form 143 (TCS)

A workspace for CAs

Every client company and its filing status on one screen. Roles for owner, accountant and read-only auditor. Auditors raise queries on any voucher, and there's a compliance calendar and bank reconciliation.

Basic or Advanced

The same switch as our STAY BAY suite. Basic shows only invoices, receipts, payments, expenses and reports. Advanced opens everything else.

Get paid faster

Invoices carry your logo, signature and a UPI QR for the exact amount due, so customers pay by scanning. Send invoices and payment reminders on WhatsApp from ready templates.

Warnings as you type

Debit and credit totals shown live on every journal, with a pop-up if they don't match. Warnings for negative cash, overdraft, over-payment, negative stock, duplicate invoices and unusually large journals.

Never locked out

Forgot the password? A one-time recovery code, shown when the owner account is made, gets you back in (5 wrong tries lock it for 15 minutes). Backup, restore and a searchable user manual are built in.

The law, built in

It stops the mistake before you make it.

Each rule below is checked every time someone saves. The key ones, like the cash limit, gap-free numbering, filed-period locks and tamper detection, have automated tests that must pass before any change is released.

Audit trail, always onEvery change is logged and hash-chained, so any tampering shows. It can't be switched off, as Rule 3(1) of the Companies (Accounts) Rules requires.
No gaps, no deletingGap-free numbering in each financial year. Cancelling keeps the number, so the series is never broken.
Locked when filedEntries in a filed GST period, invoices with an IRN and anything before the lock date can't be changed.
Cash limitsA cash receipt of ₹2 lakh or more is refused (section 269ST); a cash payment above ₹10,000 gets a warning.
Deadlines watchedThe 30-day e-invoice reporting limit, the credit-note time limit and MSME dues past 45 days are all flagged.
Slips refusedDuplicate supplier bills, future-dated tax invoices, unbalanced journals and credit notes larger than the invoice are refused.
Checked before filingA pre-filing check lists everything to fix before you file a GST return.

My story

Why a commerce student builds software.

At CA firms I saw the same thing again and again. Businesses had good people and honest intentions, but their records lived in registers, diaries and scattered Excel sheets. Month-end meant late nights, guesswork and the occasional angry employee whose pay came out wrong.

I spent my internships filing TDS and TCS returns, preparing financial statements from scratch, scrutinising ledgers and designing income-tax salary computation worksheets. I learned exactly where the numbers go wrong, and why.
